---

For the past 3 years, I've been posting hot takes and other random tidbits about my interests on this blog.

Today, I'm pausing this practice. This decision supports my commitment to the activities that matter most to me:
- Reducing the suffering of farmed animals - a significant intellectual and emotional commitment
- Living as my true self - a significant mental, physical, and finanical commitment
- Being the most supportive wife and dog-mum that I can be - a significant time commitment!

As such, I've removed most of my 230 blog posts. I've retained the 18 posts that are most important to me. These posts illustrate the spread of concepts that reflect my identity: animal advocacy; living compassionately; navigating mental illness; social and political analysis; and one post on the mathematics of football!

I may very well return to blogging one day. But for now, I want to be ruthless in focusing on my three core goals listed above.

Peace and love <3
